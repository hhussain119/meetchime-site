Frequently asked questions

How is MeetChime different from calendar notifications?

Google Calendar and macOS show a small banner that disappears in seconds and is silenced by Focus. MeetChime shows a full-screen alert with a chime that grows until you respond, and a button that joins the call directly.

Which calendars does it work with?

Google Calendar today, including Google Workspace accounts. Outlook and iCloud are on the roadmap.

Which meetings ring?

By default, meetings with a video link and at least one other person, that you haven't declined. You can turn any meeting on or off with one click, for a single occurrence or every time it repeats.

Does it help with ADHD, hyperfocus or time blindness?

Many people miss meetings because they're deep in focus and a small notification doesn't register. MeetChime was built for exactly that moment: the full-screen alert interrupts your focus right when the meeting starts, so you don't have to keep an eye on the clock.

Will it ring if my Mac is asleep?

A sleeping Mac can't ring. As soon as you wake it, MeetChime shows any meeting that's already started so you can jump straight in. A phone fallback for when your laptop is closed is on the roadmap.

Does it work with Focus and Do Not Disturb?

Yes. Focus silences notifications, but MeetChime's alert is its own full-screen window, so it still gets through.

What does MeetChime do with my calendar data?

It reads your events to know when to ring, and keeps them on your Mac. Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy for details.
